# Overview
Welcome to KingTech Labs.

KingTech Labs is where we document and share our most recent projects.
This blog follows our journey as we experiment with exotic hardware, complex software, and everything in between.

![Home Lab](https://res.cloudinary.com/kingtech/image/upload/q_auto/f_auto/v1775759066/kingtech_labs_zp1qtt.png)


## Home Lab
A big part of KingTech Labs revolves around our home lab. This server rack hosts servers, networking equipment, storage, and more.

Our environment hosts a mix of services, websites, and game servers, and each project teaches us something new.
From hardware choices to orchestration and day-to-day operations, we share practical notes so others can learn from what works, what breaks, and how we keep everything running.

## Tutorials
At KingTech, we believe in sharing knowledge. That is why we write up extensive tutorials such that you can repeat our experiments at home. We also give tips and tricks on how to make improvements and adapt our findings to your needs. 

Looking for a LAN server? Want to set up a distributed web server? Look no further! KingTech Labs has everything you need.
