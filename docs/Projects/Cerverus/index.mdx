The heart of our homelab is Cerverus, our main server. Cerverus is a custom build server meant to run our different [services](../../Services/index.mdx). 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/kingtech/image/upload/q_auto/f_auto/v1776020551/PXL_20260410_132952871_honyvf.jpg)

For this server we chose [Proxmox](https://www.proxmox.com/) as our operating system. This operating systems allows us to experiment with virtual machines without needing physical access to it. In contrast to VMWare's VSphere (ESXI) it is completely free and open-source, making it ideal for our experiments.

The original Cerverus build was recorded for our youtube channel and can be viewed in the video below. However, as we keep advancing our setup, the video is slightly outdated. All the latest improvements can be found in the KingTech Labs blogs.
