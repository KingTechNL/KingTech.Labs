# Projects

![Projects](https://res.cloudinary.com/kingtech/image/upload/q_auto/f_auto/v1775756464/kingtech_projects_rplei4.png)

At KingTech, we love our projects. Our 'home lab' is where we do most of our experiments, but we like to take on all kinds of challendes. Our projects are where hardware, software, and creativity come together into something tangible.

Some projects solve real problems, others are pure experiments, built to learn something new, push a piece of hardware to its limits, or simply because it seemed like a fun challenge.

What you will find here are write-ups of the things we built, complete with the reasoning behind design choices, the steps we took to get there, and the lessons the process taught us. Not every project goes perfectly, and that is often where the most interesting documentation comes from.
