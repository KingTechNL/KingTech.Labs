# High availability

As we are running our LXC container and virtual machines in a proxmox cluster, we can set it up for 'high availability'. This means that, when the machine the LXC/VM runs on goes down, the LXC/VM is automatically migrated to another node.



## Step 1: Add the Container to HA Resources
 1. Go to **Datacenter** -> **HA** -> **Resources** tab.
 1. Click **Add**.
 1. Click the VM/CT dropdown. Your Cloudflare Tunnel LXC ID will be visible here. Select it.
 1. Set the Max. Restart to 1 and Max. Relocate to 1.
 1. Ensure Requested State is set to started.
 1. Click **Add**

## Step 2: Configure the Node Affinity Rule
Now that the container is an active HA resource, the rules engine can interact with it.
 1. Click on the main Datacenter folder at the top of your left resource tree.
 1. Navigate to the HA section -> click on the Rules tab.
 1. Click the Create / Add button and choose Node Affinity Rule.
 1. Fill out the rule parameters:
    1. **Comment/Name:** Type tunnel-priority so you can identify it.
    1. **Nodes:** Select your three cluster nodes and declare their numerical priority (higher numbers represent your preferred primary nodes):
        * **Node 1:** Priority 3 (Main preferred host)
        * **Node 2:** Priority 2 (First backup)
        * **Node 3:** Priority 1 (Last resort)
    1. **Resources:** Look for your Cloudflare LXC ID in the drop-down selection and check the box next to it to attach it to this rule.
 1. Click Create.
